## C. Replays with one injected change, through four routes

Each conversation replayed unchanged and with one change injected at a random model call (tools reordered, a
timestamp in the system prompt, a system edit, an earlier message edited, an earlier tool result edited),
through `chat-openai` and `chat-anthropic` (litellm.acompletion) and `messages-anthropic` and
`messages-openai` (litellm.anthropic.messages, Claude Code's route; LiteLLM sends the OpenAI model the
Responses API). Claude's routes use claude-haiku-4-5, whose minimum cacheable prefix is 4,096 tokens.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

| SWE-smith | 10,992 | 10,992 | 0 | 600 | 522 | 2 | 76 | 0 |

By route (all four datasets):

| Route | named | seen, cost nothing | below minimum | missed | false alarms |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| chat-openai | 475 | 2 | 98 | 0 | 0 |
| chat-anthropic | 435 | 0 | 140 | 0 | 0 |
| messages-anthropic | 431 | 0 | 144 | 0 | 0 |
| messages-openai | 472 | 2 | 101 | 0 | 0 |

- **Below minimum:** the turn before the change was under the model's minimum cacheable prefix (4,096 tokens on
  claude-haiku-4-5, 1,024 on OpenAI), so nothing was cacheable and nothing could be lost; `below_min_prefix` is
  the right verdict, and these 483 are no evidence either way. They are mostly APIGen-MT's short dialogues, and
  on Claude's routes the changes injected early in a conversation (history and tool-result edits are injected
  after the message they edit, so later).
- **Seen, cost nothing:** an edit to an earlier tool result in the last part of the prompt, which the read
  covered to within one 128-token reporting unit; recorded as a secondary cause, as in test C.
- Of the 1,817 changes made where something was cacheable, 1,813 were named and 4 seen at no cost; none was
  missed or named as another cause, and no unchanged turn got a change cause.

Per route and change, every line of every file, is in the four `hf-c-*.json` summaries (`summary` key).
