# Prefixwise on the FreeInference agentic trace: two weeks (2026-11-23)

`prefixwise_trace.py` at c25b050a, run by Muse in Colab on two weeks of the
[FreeInference agentic trace](https://github.com/HarvardMadSys/freeinference_agentic_trace) (Harvard MADSys, CC BY 4.0;
trace repository at 2b795dbf, tables from `harvardMadsys/freeinference_agentic_trace`). The self-test passed first
(29 verdicts, 8 token checks). The numbers below are as the script printed them. The script's full summaries are
kept next to this file: [`2026-11-23-week-2026-07-19.json`](2026-11-23-week-2026-07-19.json) and
[`2026-11-23-week-2026-08-30.json`](2026-11-23-week-2026-08-30.json) (verdicts, causes, validation tables, and
breakdowns by provider letter, model, harness and subagent).

The trace has no text: each request's input is its 16-token block hashes, with the provider's cached-token count,
timing, model, an anonymous provider letter, and the authors' own `transition` and `cause`, computed from the text
before it was anonymised. Prefixwise sees only the hashes and the counts, so the authors' labels are an independent
check of its verdicts.

## What it cannot say

No text, so a system-prompt change cannot be split into a changed timestamp or id (`volatile_system`, avoidable)
and a real edit, nor a tool change into reordering (`tool_reserialize`, avoidable) and a catalog change: those are
reported as undetermined, not guessed. Provider letters are anonymous, so cache lifetimes are unknown and a miss
after 5-60 minutes may be expiry or routing. Assumptions the trace's code could not confirm are listed in the README.
