## Reading this result

- **The prediction holds.** Turning the fix on realised 97.5% (vLLM) and 99.9% (Anthropic) of the savings Prefixwise attributed to `tool_reserialize`. That is what `prefixwise fixes` promises a user before they turn the fix on.
- **Task success is flat**, by the E4 bar of at most 5 points. On Anthropic nothing changed (0 of 60 turns). On Qwen3-4B one turn of 60 changed, and at temperature 0 that is plausibly the tool order itself rather than noise. So the honest claim is "tool order rarely matters", not "never". Small models are the ones to watch, and anyone can re-check with `experiments/e4` on their own model.
- **The size of the saving depends on where the provider renders tools.** On Anthropic, tools are the first thing in the prompt, so a reorder misses almost everything: 104,616 tokens recovered. Qwen3's chat template renders tools *after* the system prompt, so a reorder only misses the tail: 13,872 tokens. The prediction matched in both cases, because Prefixwise prices a miss from the cached tokens the provider reports, not from an assumed order.
